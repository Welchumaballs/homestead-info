Terms of use

Homestead is a private household application for invited users. This website provides information about the application and its optional Google Calendar integration; it does not provide public account registration.

Authorized use

Only connect a Google account and calendar you are authorized to use. The household administrator manages the shared connection. Users are responsible for reviewing plans, calendar sharing settings, and the information they enter into synchronized notes.

Planning and synchronization

Calendar synchronization depends on valid authorization, network access, and Google service availability. Changes may take time to appear or require a retry. Check synchronization status and important dates rather than assuming every update has arrived.

Recipes and gardening guidance are educational planning aids. Review instructions and use appropriate adult supervision for children, particularly around heat, knives, and other equipment.

Privacy and support

Use of the integration is described in the privacy policy. Google's services remain subject to Google's applicable terms. For support or access questions, contact scam.alert.notify@gmail.com.
