Meals, gardens, and household planning

Make a plan. Grow and cook together.

Homestead helps a household organize recipes, plan meals for a week or month, follow cooking instructions, and keep track of garden and homestead tasks.

Bring your plans to Google Calendar

An optional Google Calendar connection lets the household administrator send planned meals and scheduled tasks to a chosen calendar. Meal events include the date, serving count, notes when entered, and links back to the recipe and meal plan. Changes to tracked meals can update or remove their calendar events.

Homestead reads calendar details to check the connection and identify the destination. It reads its tracked meal events to synchronize changes safely. Its current Calendar permission is broad enough to view and manage calendars; its implemented sync operations target the configured calendar and tracked events.

The Calendar connection does not request Gmail or Google Drive permission. Google authorization is optional; household members can use the application without connecting their own Google accounts.

A private household application

Homestead is operated on the owner's infrastructure for invited household users. This public website explains the application and its Calendar integration. Application access is provided separately by the household administrator.

The connection is shared by the household. Calendar events are visible according to the destination calendar's Google sharing settings, so choose a calendar appropriate for the information you plan to sync.
