Privacy policy

This policy covers Homestead's Google Calendar connection and this informational website. Homestead is a private household application operated by its owner for invited users.

Information accessed and purpose

When the household administrator connects Google Calendar, Homestead receives authorization tokens and records the granted permissions. It reads calendar metadata, including the calendar identifier and information used to display and test the connection. A primary calendar identifier may contain the connected account's email address.

Homestead sends meal and task information to the selected calendar: titles, dates, descriptions, serving counts, entered notes, and links to the relevant app pages. It stores event identifiers and synchronization records and reads tracked meal events to create, update, and remove those events safely.

The requested permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar, permits broader calendar access than the individual sync operations. The current integration uses it for calendar metadata and event management. This Calendar-only connection does not request Gmail or Google Drive access.

Storage and access

Connection information and synchronization records are stored on the operator's infrastructure. OAuth client secrets and access and refresh tokens are encrypted in the application database. Database backups may retain copies under the operator's backup retention arrangements. Secrets are not returned by the connection-status interface or published on this website.

Authorized household users can see connection status and shared household plans. Only an application administrator can change the Google connection. Google stores the synchronized events, and people with access to the destination calendar may see them according to that calendar's sharing settings.

Use and sharing

Google user data is used to provide the Calendar connection and synchronization features. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. This integration does not send calendar contents to an external AI service.

Homestead's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google processes the Calendar API requests under its applicable terms and privacy policies.

Disconnecting, retention, and deletion

The administrator can use Settings → Connections → Google → Disconnect to clear the application's stored connection credentials. Google authorization can also be revoked in Google Account connections. Revoking access and disconnecting do not themselves delete existing Google Calendar events, local meal or task records, synchronization history, or backup copies.

Existing events can be removed in Google Calendar. While connected, deleting a tracked meal in Homestead queues removal of its tracked event. Local records and backups remain until removed or expired under the operator's retention arrangements. Contact the operator to request deletion of retained integration data or to discuss backup retention.

This website

This static site has no sign-in, forms, analytics scripts, advertising, or application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical request information, including IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
